Referee’s Official Report Form
You are requested to:
  1. Be at the venue one hour before the start of the game.
  2. Arrange Umpires for the Match.
  3. Complete this form and return it together with team sheets within three days of the match.
  4. Any report containing a disciplinary issue must be faxed to 01-865 8650 or e-mailed to brendan.connolly@gaa.ie no later than midday the following day.
The Referee’s report should be completed accurately, completely and honestly.
Result notification: Please text the result of the fixture to 087-6567689 no more than 1 hour after the game.

Use the official rule-book wording when recording disciplinary matters. The category list above is provided for structured entry and can be edited in Advanced Settings.
CategoryReference
Team Officials — Category IaAbusive language towards a Referee, Umpire, Linesman or Sideline Official.
Team Officials — Category IIaAny type of physical interference with an Opposing Player or Team Official.
Team Officials — Category IIIaMinor physical interference, threatening or abusive conduct, or threatening language to a Referee, Umpire, Linesman or Sideline Official.
Team Officials — Category IVaAny type of assault on a Referee, Umpire, Linesman or Sideline Official.
